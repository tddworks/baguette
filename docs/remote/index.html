Remote coding

The agent keeps working. You keep it on course.

A long task doesn’t have to keep you at your desk. Look in from the sofa, the train or the queue for coffee.

01

A terminal that fits your phone

Your Mac makes a screen the exact shape of this iPhone, so the agent’s session fills it edge to edge at Retina sharpness. No squinting at a desktop.

  • Watch files change and tests run as they happen
  • Answer a question with one tap: esc ⏎ ⌃C and the arrows float on the screen
  • On a weak connection the Basic picture keeps text sharp

02

Say the next instruction

Hold Dictate and talk. Your words are recognised on the phone and typed where the Mac’s cursor is.

  • Or type on iOS’s own keyboard, each key landing on the Mac as you press it
  • Paste from iPhone types what you copied: an error, a link, a prompt you wrote earlier
  • ⌘ ⌥ ⌃ ⇧ hold for your next key, click or scroll

04

The Mac’s own gestures

The glass is the Mac’s trackpad. Your fingers mean what they mean on a MacBook.

  • Slide to point, tap to click, two fingers to scroll and right-click
  • Pinch to zoom a map or a page, turn two fingers to rotate it
  • Three fingers up for Mission Control, sideways for desktops

05

A picture that gets through

Choose how much network to spend: Best, Light, Low or Basic.

  • Best is full video on your own Wi-Fi
  • Light and Low hold the Mac to 8 and 4 Mbps for a slower link
  • Basic needs no video at all; over Tailscale it is where you start

1

Turn on Screen Sharing

On the Mac: System Settings, General, Sharing, Screen Sharing. Nothing of ours is installed.

2

Open the app and sign in

It finds Macs on your Wi-Fi; away from home, type the Mac’s address or Tailscale name. Your password stays in this iPhone’s Keychain.

3

For simulators and phones

Run baguette serve on the Mac and they appear in the Devices tab.

Privacy

Between your phone and your Mac. Nobody else.

No accountNothing to sign up for, nothing to log in to but your own Mac.
No analyticsThe app collects no data. Your code and your screen go to your phone and nowhere else.
No relay of oursThe phone talks to the Mac directly, over your network or your own Tailscale.
