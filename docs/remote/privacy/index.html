Privacy Policy

Baguette Remote for iPhone and iPad · Last updated 28 November 2026

Baguette Remote collects no personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your computer is reached directly over your own network; nothing you see or type passes through a server of ours — we don't run one.

What stays on your device

  • Your computer's account name and password, when you sign in to its screen sharing — kept in this device's Keychain, used only to sign in to that computer, never sent anywhere else.
  • Your computers: their names and addresses, and the settings you choose for each.
  • The last picture of each computer's screen, small, so its card on the home screen shows it — kept in the app's cache on this device, deleted when you forget that computer.
  • Your preferences: background, app icon, shortcuts, tips already shown.

Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What goes to your own computer

The app connects only to computers you choose — directly, over your Wi-Fi, a cable, or your own VPN such as Tailscale. It sends your touches, keys, typed and dictated text there, and receives the computer's screen and sound. That connection is between your device and your computer only.

Local network

iOS asks your permission for the app to use the local network. The app uses it only to find computers with screen sharing on near you and to connect to the ones you choose.

Dictation

When you dictate, your speech is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition. Where your device supports it, this happens on the device; otherwise Apple processes the audio under Apple's privacy policy. The app keeps no recording. iOS asks your permission before the microphone and speech recognition are used.

Face ID

If you turn on Unlock with Face ID for a computer, iOS checks your face and tells the app only whether it matched. The app never sees or stores face data.

Purchases

Baguette Remote Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment and your Apple Account; the app only learns whether Pro is active. Subscriptions are managed in your Apple Account settings.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
