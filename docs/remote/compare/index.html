Beyond the Mac's desktop

The Mac, and everything plugged into it.

Every app here shows a Mac. Baguette Remote also lists the simulators, emulators and phones that Mac is running, in one place, and you touch them directly, with no pointer in the way.

iOS simulators

The Mac's iPhone and iPad simulators, listed on your phone. Boot one, shut it down, and tap, slide and type into it like the real thing.

Android emulators

The Android emulators on the same Mac sit in the same list. Try your Android build from the sofa, next to the iOS one.

Real iPhones on a cable

A Mac mini on the desk with test iPhones plugged in: each phone streams to yours, and you touch it from wherever you are.

These need baguette serve on the Mac: baguette is open source, installed with brew install baguette (Xcode 26 on Apple silicon; the Android SDK for emulators). The Mac's own screen needs nothing installed. None of the other apps' pages mention simulators, emulators or plugged-in iPhones.

The apps

Each one, in a few lines.

Baguette Remote

tddworks · free public beta

Your Mac on iPhone and iPad through macOS's own Screen Sharing, in a screen the iPhone's shape, with the keys a coding agent asks for. With baguette on the Mac, its simulators, Android emulators and plugged-in iPhones too.

baguette.tddworks.com/remote →

Yosei

Tomas Truben · free beta

Your Mac on iPhone and iPad through its own Mac app, in a display shaped to the device. Up to 120 fps on ProMotion iPads, sound both ways, and an Agents screen.

yosei.app →

Ferry Remote Desktop

App Store · free download

For iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV. macOS Screen Sharing and VNC, High Performance sessions, virtual displays sized to the device, Picture in Picture, file transfer and SSH.

App Store →

Screens

Edovia · 7-day free trial

For iPhone, iPad, Mac and Vision Pro, reaching Macs, Windows, Linux and Raspberry Pi. High Performance Screen Sharing, Screens Connect for the internet, Tailscale and SSH.

edovia.com/en/screens →

OpenScreens

burakgon · free, MIT

Open source for Mac, iPhone and iPad. High Performance mode (HEVC, up to 60 fps, sound), VNC for Windows and Linux, built-in Tailscale, SSH tunnels and virtual displays.

github.com/burakgon/OpenScreens →

Open Screen Sharing

nekohasekai · GPL-3.0

Open source for iOS, macOS and Android, "optimized for Mac screen sharing and public networks," with standard VNC and RDP. $1.99 on the App Store.

github.com/nekohasekai/open-screen-sharing →
